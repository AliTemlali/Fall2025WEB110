Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I water container plants?

Most containers dry out faster than garden beds. In summer, daily watering may be needed.

Which soil should I use?

Always choose a high-quality potting mix, never garden soil.

Can I grow tomatoes on a balcony?

Yes — choose dwarf or patio varieties and ensure 6–8 hours of sun.

What size pots are best?

Larger containers retain moisture longer and allow roots to grow freely.

How do I prevent pests?

Inspect weekly, use neem oil, and avoid overwatering.
